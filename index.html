Questions

Good to know before you visit.

Is a root canal painful?

With local anaesthesia, most patients are comfortable during treatment. Your dentist will explain what to expect and how to manage any soreness afterwards.

How often should I get a check-up?

Usually every six months. Your dentist may suggest a different schedule based on your gum and tooth health.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We see children for check-ups, cleaning and preventive care, and we take time to help them feel at ease.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call the clinic on [PHONE NUMBER] as soon as you can. For severe swelling, heavy bleeding or injury to the face, go to the nearest hospital.
